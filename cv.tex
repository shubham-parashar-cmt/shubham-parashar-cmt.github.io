\cvsection{Research focus}
\fontsize{10.6}{13.3}\selectfont
Theoretical condensed-matter physicist working on quantum transport, kinetic theory, electron hydrodynamics, magnetotransport, and quantum Hall systems. I study a general inference problem: when finite-resolution maps of voltage, current, and dissipation can distinguish microscopic kinetic dynamics from an effective fluid description.

\cvsection{Selected research capabilities}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.15em,itemsep=6.0pt,topsep=0pt,parsep=0pt]
  \item \textbf{Analytical kinetic transport theory.} Angular-harmonic expansions of the Boltzmann equation, higher-gradient and finite-wave-number response, and controlled hydrodynamic closures.
  \item \textbf{Symmetry-designed observables.} Drive and device symmetries that suppress leading dissipative channels locally and isolate higher kinetic harmonics as measurable signals.
  \item \textbf{Finite-resolution transport inference.} Spatial voltage, current, and dissipation maps, thermoelectric and Hall contrast, and finite-resolution scanning-probe inference.
\end{itemize}
