\cvsection{Research profile}
\fontsize{9.7}{11.5}\selectfont
Theoretical condensed-matter physicist working on quantum transport, kinetic theory, electron hydrodynamics, magnetotransport, spatially resolved response, and quantum Hall systems. I develop controlled analytical and computational descriptions that connect microscopic kinetic structure to experimentally accessible observables and test where low-moment hydrodynamic closures cease to be complete.

\cvsection{Selected research contributions}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.15em,itemsep=1.6pt,topsep=0pt,parsep=0pt]
  \item \textbf{Kinetic transport beyond hydrodynamic closure.} Derived higher-gradient and finite-wave-number structure that exposes angular harmonics omitted by stress-level hydrodynamics, including a fourth-order closure obstruction and its chiral nonlocal completion.
  \item \textbf{Symmetry-selected kinetic observables.} Constructed a sixfold-driven local hydrodynamic null in which ordinary Ohmic and rank-two viscous dissipation vanish at the symmetry point while a higher kinetic harmonic survives as the leading local dissipative signal.
  \item \textbf{Finite-resolution transport inference.} Developed observable-level tests for thermoelectric Hall contrast and Hall-viscous response in scanning-probe measurements while accounting for boundary, higher-gradient, and finite-resolution nuisance channels.
\end{itemize}
