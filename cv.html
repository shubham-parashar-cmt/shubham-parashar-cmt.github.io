Research Profile

Theoretical condensed-matter physicist developing analytical and computational methods for spatially resolved transport in two-dimensional electron systems. My work connects Boltzmann kinetics, hydrodynamics, magnetotransport, scanning-probe observables, and inverse identifiability, with applications to graphene and quantum Hall systems.

Research Interests

Quantum transport; electron hydrodynamics; semiclassical kinetic theory; magnetotransport; graphene and two-dimensional electron gases; quantum Hall scanning-probe response; inverse problems and observability; higher-gradient transport closures.

Selected Research Contributions

  • Derived analytical and numerical magnetotransport response around smooth density perturbations across Ohmic and hydrodynamic regimes.
  • Developed semiclassical Boltzmann calculations used to interpret graphene scanning-tunneling potentiometry near a circular p–n junction.
  • Identified the fourth-order current operator omitted by stress-level hydrodynamic closure and its magnetic-field completion into chiral finite-wave-number response.
  • Formulated transport-functional and covariance-weighted tests separating thermoelectric, Hall-viscous, boundary, and higher-gradient contributions in scanning-probe images.
  • Derived a small-denominator mechanism for heavy-tailed light-inertial-particle trajectory geometry.
