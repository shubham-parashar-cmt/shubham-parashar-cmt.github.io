## 1. CORE PRINCIPLE

The site and CV must simultaneously work for:

A. physics faculty seeking:
- publications,
- technical depth,
- research independence,
- talks,
- evidence of calculational ability;

B. interdisciplinary scientific/technology committees seeking:
- a comprehensible physical problem,
- measurable observables,
- experimental interfaces,
- plausible relevance outside the immediate subfield.

ACCESSIBLE PHYSICS
→ CONCRETE SCIENTIFIC RESULT
→ MEASUREMENT / OBSERVABLE INTERFACE
→ TECHNICAL EVIDENCE

→ TECHNOLOGY BUZZWORDS.
